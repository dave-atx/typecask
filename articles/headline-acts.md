Some fonts only come alive when they're big. These are for slide titles, posters, covers, and the top of a
newsletter. Pair one with a quiet font from [Workhorses](article:workhorses) for the body text, and let it do
the shouting.

## Tall and tight

@font-bebas-neue: ALL CAPS. NO APOLOGIES.

**Bebas Neue**, by Ryoichi Tsunekawa of [Dharma Type](https://dharmatype.com/), is a tall,
[condensed](term:condensed), all-caps sans serif based on his earlier Bebas. It's been on so many posters,
packages, and title cards that it may be the most recognizable free [display font](term:display-font)
around.

@font-instrument-serif: The Quiet Confidence of a Narrow Serif

**Instrument Serif** was made for [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/), a design agency, as a condensed
serif for large sizes. It takes the [old-style](term:old-style) serif somewhere modern: elegant, narrow, and a
little bit editorial.

## Geometric

@font-league-spartan: THIS. IS. SPARTAN.

**League Spartan** is [The League of Moveable Type](https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com/)'s take on
Matt Bailey's Spartan, which goes back to the American [geometric](term:geometric) sans serifs of the early
20th century (Futura's American cousins; see [Inspired by the classics](article:inspired-by-the-classics)).
Set it in its heaviest weights for maximum effect.

@font-space-grotesk: Launch Week, Day One

**Space Grotesk**, by Florian Karsten, is the proportional version of [Space Mono](font:font-space-mono),
from [Screens of the past](article:screens-of-the-past). It keeps the mono's quirky details, like the
squared-off curves, in a font made for headings and short text.

## With some attitude

@font-bricolage-grotesque: Some Assembly Required

**Bricolage Grotesque** is a collage of influences: it started as a fork of Jérémy Landes's Mayenne Sans and
mixes in French and British [grotesques](term:grotesque) like Antique Olive and Grotesque No. 9. Its designer
describes it as trying to express "what it feels like to move countries and rebuild." Bricolage is French
for do-it-yourself tinkering, and its condensed, heavy cuts are made for titles.

@font-zilla-slab: moz://a

**Zilla Slab** is Mozilla's own typeface, the one in its moz://a logo. It's a contemporary
[slab serif](term:slab-serif) from [Typotheque](https://www.typotheque.com/), based on their Tesla, with a
sturdy, slightly industrial look that's surprisingly friendly.

## Elegant

@font-cormorant: An Evening of Chamber Music

**Cormorant**, by Christian Thalmann of [Catharsis Fonts](https://github.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant), is a
display family inspired by Garamond (see [Inspired by the classics](article:inspired-by-the-classics)), but
drawn almost entirely from scratch: when he needed a reference, he searched for "Garamond" and skimmed the
results for a general impression. It has nine styles, including an upright cursive and a unicase, and its
delicate details shine at large sizes.
