Not every font has to be sensible. These are for birthday invitations, kids' projects, band posters, and
notes that should read like you're smiling. Several are [variable fonts](term:variable-font) with playful
extras, so the same font can go from calm to completely unhinged.

## Bouncy

@font-shantell-sans {BNCE=100 INFM=100}: Doodling in the margins is allowed!

**Shantell Sans** comes from the British artist [Shantell Martin](https://shantellmartin.art/), known for
drawing with words on everything from the Oculus at the World Trade Center to the screens of Times Square.
Reading and writing were a struggle for her at school, and she made this font with Stephen Nixon of
[Arrow Type](https://www.arrowtype.com/) to help people have fun with words. Its Bounce setting jiggles the
letters up and down, and its Informality setting goes from tidy marker to full scribble.

@font-recursive {CASL=1}: Painted by a sign painter, typed by you

**Recursive** is also from Arrow Type. It's inspired by single-stroke casual, the brush lettering sign
painters use, and one font goes all the way from a crisp sans serif to a loose, brushy Casual, and from
proportional to [monospaced](term:monospaced) for code.

## Wonky

@font-fraunces {SOFT=100 WONK=1}: Wonky, goofy and proud of it

**Fraunces**, by Phaedra Charles and Flavia Zimbardi of [Undercase Type](https://undercase.xyz/), is a soft,
chunky [old-style](term:old-style) serif in the spirit of Windsor, Souvenir, and Cooper, the fonts of 1970s
album covers. Its Softness setting makes the letters look inkier, and its Wonky setting swaps in letters that
lean, like the h, n, and m. It's playful, but tame it and it's a fine headline font too.

## In color

@font-nabla: LEVEL UP!

**Nabla** is a [color font](term:color-font) inspired by isometric video games, with gradients, highlights,
and shadows built into the letters. [Arthur Reinders Folmer](https://typearture.com/) drew it, and Just van
Rossum (Python creator Guido's brother) did the font engineering. Typecask shows it in full color.

## Handwriting

@font-playwrite-us-trad: Dear Grandma, thank you for the sweater

**[Playwrite](font:font-playwrite-us-trad)** is a collection of the handwriting styles children are taught
at school, made by [TypeTogether](https://www.type-together.com/) (who also made Literata, from
[Workhorses](article:workhorses)) for Google. The catalog has 52 of them, from Argentina to Vietnam, plus a
[Guides](font:font-playwrite-us-trad-guides) version of each with ruled practice lines, which makes them
perfect for homemade worksheets.

@font-fantasque-sans-mono: let mood = "wibbly-wobbly";

**Fantasque Sans Mono**, by [Jany Belluz](https://github.com/belluzj/fantasque-sans), is a coding font with
"some wibbly-wobbly handwriting-like fuzziness." It used to be called Cosmic Sans Neue Mono, because its
designer thought it looked like "the mutant child of Comic Sans and Helvetica Neue," but people's feelings
about Comic Sans rubbed off on it, so it got a new name.
