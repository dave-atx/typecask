Typecask collects nothing about you, and the app has no way to send anything anywhere. This page
covers the app and this website separately, because they're different things with different
behaviour.

## The app

**Typecask collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or crash
reporting SDKs, and no advertising.

**The app makes no network requests of its own.** Every font is bundled with the app as an
On-Demand Resource, which means installing one downloads it from Apple's App Store servers the same
way any other app content arrives. Apple sees that request; Typecask doesn't, and there's no server
of ours in the path. Apple's handling of it is covered by the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**What stays on your device.** The app records which fonts you've installed and their versions, so
it can offer Update when a later release carries a newer version. That record lives on your device.
It isn't sent anywhere and isn't readable by us.

**Fonts you install become system fonts.** They appear in
Settings&nbsp;&rsaquo;&nbsp;General&nbsp;&rsaquo;&nbsp;Fonts and in any app that can use installed
fonts. You can remove them from Typecask or from Settings.

## This website

**Page views are counted, without cookies.** typecask.app uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets
no cookies, stores no identifier in your browser, and doesn't follow you to other sites. It records
the page you looked at, the referrer, and your browser, device type and country, derived from the
request. Cloudflare's
[Web Analytics privacy documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/)
describes what it collects.

**There are no other trackers.** No advertising, no session recording, no third-party embeds. Fonts,
styles and scripts are served from typecask.app itself, so loading a page makes no request to anyone
else.

**Server logs.** Cloudflare, which hosts the site, processes requests and keeps short-lived logs as
part of delivering it.

## Children

Typecask is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, children included.
